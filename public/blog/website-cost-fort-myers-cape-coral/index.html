What Does a Small Business Website Cost in Fort Myers and Cape Coral?

By Daniel · November 15, 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer

A small business website can cost anything from a monthly builder subscription you set up yourself to $10,000 or more from an agency. Freelancers usually land in between. My own fixed prices are $500 for a one-page Starter site and $1,500 for a Business site of up to five pages, with custom quotes for stores and booking systems. After launch, budget about $10 to $20 a year for a domain; hosting on Cloudflare is often free.

Ask around in Fort Myers or Cape Coral what a website costs and you'll hear numbers from "free" to "more than my truck". Both can be true. Here's how the options break down, what moves the price, and where I fit.

The three main routes

Do it yourself with a builder

Wix, Squarespace and similar tools charge a monthly fee, usually tens of dollars a month for a business plan. The real cost is your time: choosing a template, writing every word, sorting photos and learning enough about search to be found. It can work well for a simple business with time to spare. It tends to stall when you're busy, which in season is always.

Hire a freelancer

Freelance prices vary hugely, from a few hundred dollars to several thousand. The difference is usually how much is included: writing, local search setup, a mobile design that's actually tested, and whether you own the result. Always ask for a fixed price and a list of what it covers.

Hire an agency

Agencies typically start in the several thousands and can reach $10,000 or more for a custom site, often with a monthly retainer for marketing. That makes sense for larger companies running ads and campaigns. Most small businesses don't need that much machinery.

What moves the price

  • Number of pages. A one-page site and a twenty-page site are different jobs.
  • Writing. Who writes the words is often the biggest hidden cost.
  • Features. Online stores, booking engines, member areas and integrations add time.
  • Photos. Real photos of your work beat stock images, but a photo shoot costs extra.
  • Languages. A Spanish version of key pages adds writing and setup.

Running costs after launch

A domain name is usually $10 to $20 a year. Hosting ranges from free to tens of dollars a month depending on the platform. Sites I build run on Cloudflare in your own account, where most small business sites cost nothing or a few dollars a month. Builders bundle hosting into the subscription, so you keep paying as long as the site exists.

My prices

  • Starter, $500: one page, your words tidied up, one round of changes, about two weeks.
  • Business, $1,500: up to five pages, words written with you, a quote or booking form, local search setup, two rounds of changes, about four weeks.
  • Custom: stores, booking systems, bigger sites and brand work, quoted at a fixed price after a short call.

Half is due when we start and half at launch. You own the domain, code and hosting from day one. The pricing page has the details.

Questions to ask anyone you hire

  • Is the price fixed, and what exactly does it include?
  • Who writes the words?
  • Will the domain and hosting be in my name?
  • What will I pay each year after launch?
  • Can I update hours and prices myself?
  • What happens if we stop working together?

Clear answers to those six questions will tell you more than any portfolio.
