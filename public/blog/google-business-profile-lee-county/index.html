Google Business Profile Checklist for Lee County Small Businesses

By Daniel · November 15, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer

To show up in Lee County map results, claim and verify your Google Business Profile, choose the most specific primary category, set an accurate service area or address, keep regular and special hours current, list your services, add real photos, and match your business name, phone and address to your website exactly. Then ask happy customers for reviews and reply to every one.

When someone in Cape Coral or Fort Myers searches for a service on their phone, the map results usually appear first. Those come from Google Business Profiles. For most local businesses, this free listing does more work than any other part of their online presence. Here's how to get it right.

Set it up properly

  • Claim and verify it. Search for your business on Google Maps. If a listing exists, claim it; if not, create one. Google will verify that you're the owner, often by video or phone.
  • Use your real business name. No added keywords like "Best AC Repair Fort Myers". Google can suspend listings for that.
  • Pick the most specific primary category. "Pool cleaning service" rather than "Contractor". You can add a few secondary categories for other things you do.
  • Address or service area. If customers visit you, show your address. If you go to them, hide it and list the towns you serve, such as Cape Coral, Fort Myers and Lehigh Acres.

Fill in everything

  • Hours, plus special hours for holidays, seasonal changes and storm closures.
  • Services, listed one by one with a short description.
  • Description, written plainly: what you do, who for and where.
  • Photos of your real work, team, vehicles and storefront. Add new ones every few months.
  • Website link to the most relevant page, and a booking link if you use one.

Match your website

Your business name, phone number and address (if shown) should read exactly the same on your Google profile, your website and anywhere else you're listed. Google uses that consistency as a sign your business is real and settled. Your website should also name the same towns and services as your profile.

Reviews

  • Ask every happy customer, ideally right after the job, with a short direct link.
  • Reply to every review, good or bad, briefly and politely.
  • Never buy reviews, review your own business or offer rewards for reviews. Google removes them and can penalize the listing.

Keep it current

A profile is never finished. Update hours before season and summer, post when you add a service, and check for edits suggested by the public, which Google sometimes applies without asking. If you'd like help, local SEO setup is included with every site I build.
