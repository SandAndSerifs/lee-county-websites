Running a Seasonal Business Website in Southwest Florida

By Daniel · November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer

A seasonal business in Southwest Florida should update its website and Google Business Profile twice a year: before the winter rush, with longer hours, booking details and visitor information, and before the summer slowdown, with reduced hours and offers aimed at year round residents. Date your notices, keep hours identical everywhere, and set things up so you can make these changes yourself in minutes.

Anyone who runs a business in Lee County knows the rhythm. Traffic on US 41 builds after the holidays, reservations fill up, and by late spring things slowly ease off again until the summer quiet. Your website should follow the same rhythm instead of saying the same thing all year.

Before the winter rush

  • Update hours everywhere. Website, Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, Facebook and any booking tools. Mismatched hours are one of the quickest ways to lose a visitor's trust.
  • Write for visitors. Seasonal residents and tourists don't know the area like locals do. Add a short line on where you are relative to landmarks people recognize, parking, and whether you take walk-ins or reservations.
  • Make booking easy. If you get busy, a booking link or a simple request form saves your phone from ringing all day.
  • Refresh photos. New menu items, recent work or this year's team.

Before the summer slowdown

  • Change your hours with a date. "Summer hours from May 1" is clearer than just new numbers.
  • Talk to year round residents. Local discounts, summer specials or a loyalty offer give neighbors a reason to come in when the crowds are gone.
  • Do the work you couldn't in season. Summer is the time to fix your website, add a service page or redesign, so it's ready before the next rush.

Keep it in one place

The less you have to remember, the more likely it gets done. I set up the things that change by season, like hours, menus, specials and rates, so you can edit them from your phone. Your Google profile has a separate special hours feature for holidays, which is worth filling in every year.

A small calendar

  • Early December: season hours, holiday special hours, booking details.
  • Spring: check reviews and reply to anything unanswered.
  • May: summer hours and resident offers.
  • June: hurricane season prep (see my storm checklist).
  • Summer: fix and improve the site.
