Questions

The things people ask before they book.

Before your trip

Do we need fishing licenses?

No. Your licenses are covered by the boat's charter license.

Can kids come?

Yes. Kids of any age are welcome, and we have life jackets in every size.

What if someone gets seasick?

Inshore trips stay in calm water. If you're worried, ask about the half day.

Can we keep the fish?

Yes, within Florida's limits. We clean and bag them at the dock.

Where do we meet?

We send the meeting spot with your booking confirmation.
