Pricing questions

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. A free 20-minute chat, then one written price for the whole job, and that's the bill. Anything new you ask for later gets priced before I touch it.

How do I pay?

Two invoices: 50% to start and 50% when the site goes live. Care plans are invoiced each month.

Who writes the words?

On a Business site, I draft the copy after a half-hour interview, then we refine it together. On a Starter site, send me whatever text you've got and I'll polish it.

What will I pay each year after launch?

Roughly $10 to $20 for the domain. Hosting lives on a Cloudflare account registered to you, and most small business sites pay nothing or a couple of dollars monthly.

What if my site needs more than five pages?

Then it's a Custom project: a written plan and one flat price, normally within two days of our chat.

Who owns the finished site?

You do. The domain, code and hosting are registered to you from the start, and every login is yours on launch day.
