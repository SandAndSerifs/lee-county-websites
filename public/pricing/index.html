Pricing questions

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. After a free 20-minute call you get a written plan and one price. That's what you pay. If you later want something outside the plan, I quote it before doing any of it.

How do I pay?

Half when we start and half at launch, by invoice. Care plans are billed monthly.

Who writes the words?

For a Business site, I draft them from a half-hour interview and we edit together. For a Starter site, you send what you have and I tidy it up.

What will I pay each year after launch?

Usually $10 to $20 for your domain. Hosting runs on a Cloudflare account in your name and is free or a few dollars a month for most small business sites.

What if I need more than five pages?

That's a Custom project. You get a written plan and a fixed quote, usually within two days of our call.

Do I own the site?

Yes. Domain, code and hosting are in your name from day one, and I hand over every login at launch.
