Privacy

Here's everything this site gathers about visitors and where it goes. The short version: whatever you choose to send through the form, and, when analytics is on, anonymous numbers about visits.

Messages you send

The contact form passes along the details you enter (name, email, business name, website and your message) to my inbox through Resend, an email delivery service. I read them so I can answer you and price your project, and that's all. Your details are never sold, never passed to marketers and never added to a newsletter.

Analytics

Google Tag Manager may load Google Analytics here so I can see how many people visit and which pages they find helpful. Analytics uses cookies and collects things like the pages viewed, a rough location and your device type. I look at the totals, not at individuals, and your browser's privacy controls can switch it off.

Where the site runs

Cloudflare serves these pages. To do that and to keep out attacks, it handles basic connection details such as your IP address.

Your information

If you'd like to know what I have on file about you, or want it removed, send me a message.
