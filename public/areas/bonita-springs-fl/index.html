Doing business in Bonita Springs

Bonita Springs sits between Fort Myers and Naples, and its customers come from both directions. Many of them have high expectations and very little patience for a clunky website.

Customers from both sides of the county line

Being in the far south of Lee County means plenty of your customers live in Collier County, just across the line. If you serve both, the site should say so: Bonita Springs, Estero and the parts of Naples you'll travel to. That one clear sentence keeps you in front of people searching from either side.

Polish matters here

Bonita has a lot of golf and gated communities, a busy wellness and medical scene, and dining and shops around the old downtown along the Imperial River. Customers compare options carefully. A clean design with good photos, clear prices or price ranges, and easy booking does a lot of the selling before anyone calls.

An example of how I'd approach it

Say a made-up massage and skincare studio near Bonita Beach Road. The site would show the treatments with lengths and prices, a gallery of the actual rooms, gift cards for visiting family, and a booking button that goes straight to their scheduling tool. A short page for seasonal residents explains how to book ahead for the busy months.
