Doing business in Lehigh Acres

Lehigh Acres is a huge, fast-growing community east of Fort Myers, with new homes on what used to be empty lots and plenty of contractors, landscapers and local services working to keep up.

Trades and services first

A lot of Lehigh's businesses are owner operated: general contractors, concrete and pavers, lawn care, fencing, mobile mechanics, cleaners and handymen. Many get most of their work from referrals and Facebook groups, which works until a customer looks you up and finds nothing. A simple, professional site with your services, photos of your work and a quote form turns that word of mouth into a call.

Speak your customers' language

Many Lehigh Acres households are more comfortable in Spanish. If that's true of your customers, a site with Spanish versions of the key pages is easy to set up and tells people straight away that you'll understand them. I build them as proper pages Google can show to Spanish searchers, not an automatic translate button.

What that could look like

Take a pavers and driveway company. Its home page leads with a photo of a finished driveway, the towns covered (Lehigh Acres, Fort Myers, Cape Coral), and a quote form that asks for the size of the job and a couple of photos. A gallery page shows before and after pairs, and each page has an English and Spanish version.
