Doing business in Pine Island

Pine Island is Old Florida on purpose: fruit groves and plant nurseries along Stringfellow Road, fishing villages at either end, and the painted galleries of Matlacha on the way in. Businesses here are small and personal, and their websites should feel the same.

Two very different customers

Plenty of Pine Island businesses sell to two groups at once. Landscapers and homeowners from across the county come for palms, tropical plants and fruit trees, and they want to know what's in stock and whether you deliver. Day trippers come for Matlacha's galleries, a seafood lunch and a few hours fishing off the bridge locals call the "fishingest bridge in the world", and they want to know what's open today. One site can serve both if it's organized around those two questions.

Where on the island you are

The island has several communities, and people don't always know them apart. Bokeelia and Pineland sit up north, Pine Island Center is in the middle, and St. James City, the hub for fishing and watersports, is at the south tip. Matlacha is on its own small island along Pine Island Road, the way in from Cape Coral. Saying which one you're in, and how long the drive is from the Matlacha bridge, saves a visitor a wrong turn.

The island was hit hard by Hurricane Ian in 2022, and not everything has reopened. A current "we're open" line with this season's hours is worth more here than most anywhere.

What that could look like

Take a tropical fruit nursery off Stringfellow Road. The home page would show what's ripe or in stock this month (mango, lychee, avocado trees, palms) on a list the owner can update from a phone, the farm stand hours, and whether they deliver to Cape Coral and Fort Myers. A second page covers wholesale orders for landscapers, with a simple request form.

A Pine Island site should have

  • Which island community you're in
  • What's in stock or on the wall this month
  • Hours you can change by season
  • Delivery or wholesale details for mainland buyers
  • Photos of your real place and work
