Doing business in Sanibel

Most of a Sanibel business's customers make up their mind before they cross the causeway. Your website is where they decide, often months ahead and on a phone in another state.

Selling to people who aren't here yet

Sanibel is known for shelling, beaches and the J.N. "Ding" Darling National Wildlife Refuge, and the people who come for those things plan carefully. They compare rentals, tours and places to eat from home. The questions they ask are practical: is it open, what does it cost, how do I book, and how far is it from where I'm staying. A site that answers those quickly gets the booking.

Show that you're open

Since Hurricane Ian in 2022, many island businesses have rebuilt, reopened or moved. Visitors still wonder what's open. A clear "open daily" line with current hours, recent photos and an updated Google Business Profile removes that doubt, and it's worth checking every season.

What that could look like

Consider a beach cottage rental near the lighthouse end of the island. The site would lead with photos, the number of guests and bedrooms, rates by season and an availability calendar or booking link. A short local guide covers parking, bikes and the best shelling times, which also gives Google more reasons to show the page.

A Sanibel site should have

  • Open status and current hours, always
  • Prices or seasonal rates you can update
  • Booking or availability on the first screen
  • Recent photos of the real place
  • Practical visitor info: parking, bikes, directions
