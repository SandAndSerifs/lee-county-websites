Doing business in Fort Myers

Fort Myers has two audiences at once: people who live here year round and a winter wave of visitors and seasonal residents. A good site speaks to both without making either one dig for the phone number.

Two kinds of customer, one website

From January into April the city fills up with seasonal residents, spring training crowds heading to JetBlue Park and Hammond Stadium, and visitors staying near the River District. The rest of the year your customers are mostly neighbors. Those groups search differently. Visitors search "near me" and want hours, directions and whether you take walk-ins. Locals search by service and want to know you're reliable.

So a Fort Myers site needs hours that are easy to change by season, a short answer to "are you near me" for people who don't know the roads yet, and service pages that give locals a reason to call you rather than the next listing.

Businesses I build for in Fort Myers

  • Restaurants, cafés and shops downtown and along McGregor Boulevard, where hours, menus and parking questions come first.
  • Home services like air conditioning, pool care, roofing and pest control, where jobs go to whoever has a quick quote form and a plain list of the areas covered.
  • Medical, dental and wellness practices that need clear new patient information and a simple way to book.
  • Tour, rental and event businesses selling to visitors who plan from their phone.

How I'd approach it

Take an example: an air conditioning company based off Colonial Boulevard. In August, a homeowner with a dead unit is searching on a phone in a hot house. The site I'd build opens with a big tap-to-call button, "same day service in Fort Myers, Cape Coral and Lehigh Acres", and a short form for people who'd rather text. Each main service gets its own page, and the Google Business Profile lists the same towns, word for word.

A Fort Myers site should have

  • Hours that change easily by season
  • Clear service area for locals and newcomers
  • Tap-to-call at the top of every page
  • Directions and parking for walk-in businesses
  • Business Profile that matches the site exactly
