Things to do

Morning shelling

Walk the beach at low tide for the best shells.

Paddle the back bay

Rent a kayak and look for dolphins and manatees.

Sunset on the sand

Grab two chairs from the shed and head west around 6.

Good to know

Is there parking?

Yes, one spot per cottage, right outside.

Are pets allowed?

Dogs are welcome in The Osprey, with a small fee.

What happens in a storm?

If a hurricane warning is issued, you can move your stay or get a full refund.

Plan your stay
