About this service

A website that answers the three things every visitor wants to know straight away: what you do, whether you cover their part of Lee County, and how to reach you. Built by hand, priced up front, and owned by you.

Who this is for

Contractors, pool and lawn companies, salons, restaurants, charter captains, clinics, shops and nonprofits across Lee County. Most of the people who get in touch with me have one of three problems: no website at all, a site somebody built years ago that nobody can update, or a social media page that has quietly become the only place customers can find them.

If you need an online store, bookings tied into your scheduling software, or a large site, that's a Custom project. I'll quote it after a short call.

The two packages

Starter, $500

A single, well organized page: what you do, the services you offer, your hours, the towns you cover and a contact form that sends each enquiry to your inbox. You give me the words and photos you already have, I shape them into something clear, and you get one round of changes. Plan on about two weeks.

Business, $1,500

Up to five pages, laid out around the way your customers decide. That usually means a home page, a page for each main service, an about page and a contact or booking page. I interview you for half an hour and write the first draft of the words myself, add a quote or booking form, and set up local search. You get two rounds of design changes and 30 days of fixes after launch. Plan on about four weeks.

How the build works

You see the design on a private preview link before anything is final, so there are no surprises at launch. I design in Figma, then write the site by hand and host it on Cloudflare. No page builder, no stack of plugins to keep patched, and pages that open almost instantly on a phone, even on a weak signal at the beach.

Anything you change often, like a menu, seasonal hours or a price list, I set up so you can edit it yourself without calling me.

Ready for local search on day one

Each page launches with its own title and description, structured data that tells Google and AI assistants your business name, services and the parts of Lee County you cover, a sitemap, and Google Search Console connected so we can watch which searches you start to appear in. I also go through your Google Business Profile with you, since that listing drives most map results. The local SEO page explains what that involves.

Running costs after launch

Hosting sits on a Cloudflare account in your name. For most small business sites it costs nothing or a few dollars a month. A domain name is typically $10 to $20 a year. A care plan is optional; here's what one covers if you'd rather hand the upkeep to me.

Questions

What does a small business website cost in Fort Myers?

With me it's $500 for a one-page Starter site or $1,500 for a Business site of up to five pages, at a fixed price. Stores, booking systems and bigger sites get a fixed quote after a free 20-minute call.

Do I have to write all the words?

Not for a Business site. I write the first draft from a half-hour interview and we edit it together. For a Starter site you send what you have and I tidy it up.

Can I change things myself later?

Yes, for whatever changes often, such as hours, prices or a menu. I set it up during the build and walk you through it at launch.

Do you only work in Fort Myers and Cape Coral?

No. Those are the two biggest places I serve, but I work with businesses anywhere in Lee County and beyond. Everything runs over video calls, email and a shared preview link.
