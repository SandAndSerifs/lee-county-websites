About this service

A good logo has to work at the size of a thumbnail and on the side of a box truck parked on Colonial Boulevard. I design simple marks with the colors, fonts and files to use them properly.

What you get

  • A main logo and a compact version for small spaces like a profile picture or an embroidered shirt.
  • A color palette that holds up in bright Florida sun, on a phone screen and on vinyl.
  • A font pairing for your website, menus and printed pieces.
  • Files for every job: vector files for sign shops and wraps, transparent PNGs, and a one color version.
  • A single page guide so whoever prints your next shirt or sign gets it right.

How it goes

We talk for half an hour about your customers, your competition and where the logo will live. I come back with two or three directions shown on real things like a van door, a business card and a social profile, not floating on a white page. You pick one, we refine it over two rounds, and you get the final files.

Built to work outdoors

A lot of Lee County branding lives outside: on trucks, yard signs, boats, uniforms and shopfronts that sit in full sun all year. I avoid thin lines and pale colors that disappear in glare, and I test each logo in one color so it still works as a cut vinyl decal or a stitched patch.

  • Vector, PNG and one color files

Questions

How much does a logo cost?

After a free 20-minute chat I price each brand project as one flat number, depending on how many pieces you need. It's often cheaper added to a website project.

Do I own the logo?

Yes. Once the project is paid, the logo and every file belong to you, to use however you like.

Can you freshen up my current logo instead?

Often, yes. If people already know your logo, a careful cleanup can be the better choice. I'll tell you which I'd recommend.
