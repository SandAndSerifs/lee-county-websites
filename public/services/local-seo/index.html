About this service

When someone in Cape Coral types "near me" into their phone, Google picks a handful of businesses to show. Local SEO is the plain, unglamorous work of making sure yours is one of them.

Your Google Business Profile comes first

For most local searches the map results appear above the regular links, and those come from Google Business Profiles, not websites. So I start there: the right primary category, your real service area, accurate hours (including the slow season and holidays), services, photos and a description written the way customers search. Then I make sure your name, phone number and address read exactly the same on your site and your profile.

If you work out of your truck or from home, you can hide your address and list the towns you serve instead. That's common in Lee County trades, and I'll set it up correctly.

Pages for the places you work

A roofer based in Fort Myers who also works in Lehigh Acres and Bonita Springs needs the site to say so clearly. Where it makes sense I write a short page for each main service and each town you cover, with something specific to that place on every one. Copy and paste pages with only the town name swapped don't help, and Google has said as much.

Being the answer in AI search

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers which business to call. Those tools lean on the same signals: a clear statement of what you do and where, structured data, consistent details across the web and real reviews. I write each page so the first lines answer the question a customer would ask, which is also what AI tools quote. The details are on my AI search visibility page.

Reviews, without the tricks

Reviews matter a lot for map rankings. I'll give you a short link and a simple routine for asking happy customers, plus template replies for good and bad reviews. I won't buy reviews, filter out unhappy customers or pad your listing with keywords. Those shortcuts get listings suspended.

Questions

How long does local SEO take to work?

Fixes to your Google Business Profile can show up within a few weeks. New pages usually take one to three months to settle. Anyone who promises the top spot is guessing.

Do I need an office address to show up on Google Maps?

No. Service businesses can hide their address and list the towns they serve. You do need a real location where you're based; virtual offices break Google's rules.

Is local SEO included with a website?

The basics are included with every site I build: Business Profile cleanup, structured data, Search Console and a sitemap. Town and service pages are part of the Business package or quoted separately.

Can you help with SEO if you didn't build my site?

Yes. I start by looking at your Business Profile and your site, then tell you what's worth fixing first and what it would cost.
