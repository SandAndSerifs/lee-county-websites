About this service

Your old site still brings in some calls, so a redesign shouldn't throw that away. I rebuild what isn't working, keep what is, and make sure Google follows every old page to its new home.

Signs it's time

  • The site is awkward on a phone: tiny text, buttons that are hard to tap, or a menu that doesn't open.
  • You can't update it, or the one person who could has moved on.
  • It still shows old hours, an old phone number or a storm closure notice from a season ago.
  • People call and ask questions the site should already answer.
  • It takes several seconds to load, or your browser flags it as not secure.

Keeping the traffic you have

The biggest risk in any redesign is losing search visibility you built up over years. Before I touch the design I export your pages, the searches they show up for and the sites that link to you. Every old page address gets a permanent redirect to the matching new page, and after launch I check Search Console for errors and broken links for the following month.

I wrote about the full process in how to redesign a site without losing your Google rankings.

What changes, and what stays

Your name, your reputation and whatever already works stay. A page that brings in steady calls keeps its address and its best wording. What changes is everything that gets in a customer's way: slow pages, cramped phone layouts, buried phone numbers and forms that never arrive. If your logo needs a refresh too, I can do that at the same time.

Moving off WordPress, Wix or Squarespace

Most sites I rebuild started on one of those. I move your words, photos and any page that's earning traffic, then rebuild the site by hand so it's quicker and has far less to maintain. If you'd rather stay on your current platform, tell me on the call and I'll say honestly whether a redesign there makes sense.

Questions

Will I lose my Google rankings after a redesign?

Not if it's handled properly. I keep your best pages at the same addresses where I can and redirect the rest, then watch Search Console after launch and fix anything that slips.

What does a website redesign cost?

A flat $1,500 covers up to five pages. Larger sites and stores are priced after a free 20-minute chat, with one number in writing.

Can I keep my domain name and email?

Yes. Your domain and email stay exactly as they are. Only the website itself changes.

My site is on Wix. Can you redesign it?

Yes. I bring across your content and any pages earning traffic, then rebuild the site so it loads faster and you're not tied to a monthly builder subscription.
