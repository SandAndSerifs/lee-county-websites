Website care plans for Fort Myers and Lee County businesses

My website care plans are an optional monthly service for small businesses in Fort Myers, Cape Coral and the rest of Lee County whose owners would rather not touch their own website. A plan covers small edits on request, seasonal hour and menu changes, closure notices during storms, monthly checks that the site, forms and Google listing are working, and a short monthly note on how it's doing. Plans are quoted based on how often things change, and you can stop any time.

About this service

You don't need one. But if you'd rather text me a change and forget about it, including the hours update when a storm closes you for three days, a care plan covers it.

What's covered

  • Small edits whenever you need them: prices, photos, staff, specials.
  • Seasonal changes, like winter hours when visitors arrive and summer hours when they leave.
  • Closure and reopening notices during hurricane season, on the site and on your Google Business Profile.
  • A monthly check that pages load, forms deliver and nothing is broken.
  • A short monthly note: visits, calls and form enquiries, and anything worth changing.

Who a care plan suits

Most of the Fort Myers and Cape Coral owners who ask about care plans have the same problem: the website is never urgent until it's wrong. A restaurant that changes its menu and hours twice a year, a contractor who is on job sites from dawn, a shop on Fort Myers Beach or Sanibel that lives and dies by the season. If a change sits on your list for weeks because logging in is a chore, a care plan clears that list.

If you like doing it yourself, you don't need one. Every site I build is set up so you can edit the things that change by season from your phone, and my seasonal website guide walks through what to update and when.

How a request works

Send me a text or an email: "new lunch special, summer hours from May 1, swap the photo on the home page." I make the change, usually the same day, and reply when it's live. There's no ticket system and no login for you to remember.

Because your Google Business Profile shows up above your website in most local searches, I keep it in step with the site: the same hours, the same phone number, the same services. That's the upkeep side of local SEO for Lee County businesses, and it's part of every plan.

Storm season, handled

When a storm is coming, the last thing you want is to be logging into a website. On a care plan you send me one text, and I post the closure notice, update your Google hours and put a banner on the site. When you reopen, one more text and it all changes back. My hurricane season checklist covers what to prepare either way.

What a care plan doesn't cover

A care plan keeps a working site current. New pages, a new section or a full rebuild are separate jobs with their own fixed price, so you always know the cost before I start. If your current site needs more than upkeep, the website redesign page explains how I handle that, and the pricing page lists the packages.

No lock-in

You own the domain, code and hosting from the start, so a care plan never holds anything hostage. Stop whenever you like and everything stays exactly where it is.

What's included

  • Seasonal hour and menu updates
  • Storm closure notices on site and Google
  • Google Business Profile kept in step with the site
  • Monthly uptime, form and link checks
  • A short monthly report

Questions

Do I need a care plan?

No. Every site I build is set up so you can run it yourself, and hosting is on your own account. A care plan is for people who'd rather hand it off.

How much is a care plan?

It depends on how often your site changes, so I quote it after a quick chat. You can cancel any time.

Is a care plan the same as hosting?

No. Sites I build are hosted on a Cloudflare account in your name, which for most small business sites costs nothing or a few dollars a month. A care plan is the upkeep: edits, seasonal changes and checks.

Do you offer care plans outside Fort Myers?

Yes. I look after sites for businesses anywhere in Lee County, including Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Estero and the islands. It all happens by text, email and video call.

Can I get a care plan for a site you didn't build?

Sometimes. I'd need to look at how it's built first. If it's a heavily customised WordPress site, I may suggest a different approach.
