Print design for Fort Myers and Lee County businesses

I design print pieces for small businesses in Fort Myers, Cape Coral and around Lee County: rack cards, door hangers, flyers, menus, postcards, business cards and yard signs. Each piece is set up to your printer's exact specifications and matched to your website, often with a QR code that leads to the right page. Print design is quoted per piece at a fixed price.

About this service

Rack cards in hotel lobbies, door hangers in new Cape Coral neighborhoods, menus that survive a humid patio. Print still earns its keep here, and it works best when it sends people to a website that's ready for them.

Pieces that work around here

  • Rack cards for hotel, rental and visitor center displays, aimed at people deciding what to do this week.
  • Door hangers and postcards for trades working new construction neighborhoods in Cape Coral and Lehigh Acres.
  • Menus that stay readable on a bright patio and can be updated without starting from scratch.
  • Yard signs and banners with a phone number big enough to read from a moving car.
  • Business cards and appointment cards that match everything else.

Print that sends people somewhere useful

A QR code on a rack card is only useful if it opens a page made for that person. I can point each piece to its own page, such as a seasonal offer or a booking form, so you can see in your analytics how many people actually scanned it.

Ready for your printer

Every file comes set up for the printer you use, local or online, with the right bleed, margins and color settings. I'm happy to talk to them directly so nothing gets lost in an email chain.

What's included

  • Design matched to your brand and site
  • Printer-ready files with bleed and margins
  • QR codes pointing to the right page
  • Two rounds of changes
  • Editable templates for repeat pieces

Questions

Do you print as well as design?

I design and prepare the files, then hand them to your printer, or recommend one. You settle the print bill with them yourself, so I add no markup.

How much do rack cards or door hangers cost to design?

Each piece gets a fixed quote after a quick call. Pieces designed alongside a website or logo usually cost less.

Can you redo a menu I already have?

Yes. Send me the current one and I'll redesign it so it's easier to read and easier for you to update next season.
